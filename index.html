How I solve problems

I begin with the real workflow, clarify data definitions and exceptions, build a small testable solution, and document errors and improvements. I focus on meaningful reporting rather than dashboards alone.

For health applications, this means protecting sensitive information, using access controls, testing outputs and distinguishing exploratory models from clinical decisions.
